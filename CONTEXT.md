# Shut the Box

The office's daily game of Shut the Box: who played, who won, and everything the team keeps
score of around it (ratings, badges, seasons, and who buys the fika).

## Language

### The game

**Game**:
One sitting at the physical box, where every player takes one turn and the lowest score wins. At
most one game counts per day.
_Avoid_: Match, round, session

**Counted game**:
A game that was finished and has not been deleted. It is the day's game, and the only kind that
reaches the stats.
_Avoid_: Valid game, real game

**Abandoned game**:
A game that was started and given up before it was crowned. It never counts, and it does not take
the day.

**Turn**:
One player's go at the box, ending in a score: the sum of the tiles still standing.
_Avoid_: Round, roll

**Shut box**:
A turn that ends with every tile down, for a score of 0. It is a very good turn, not the end of the
game: everybody after it still plays.
_Avoid_: Instant win

**Boxed out**:
A player who was at the table but never got a turn because someone shut the box first. This only
happened under the old rule that a shut box ended the game, so it survives in older games only.
_Avoid_: Did not play, no-show

**Winner**:
The player with the lowest score in a game. Players who tie share the win, and all their anthems
play at once.

**Day win**:
Winning the day's game. Streaks, the monthly title and the season standings count day wins.

**Scorekeeper**:
The one phone that drives a game; everyone else watches. Anyone can take over when the
scorekeeper's phone has gone.
_Avoid_: Host, admin

### Seasons and rules

**Season**:
A calendar quarter of games, played under one ruleset and ending in a champion.

**Ruleset**:
The rules a season is played by: the board, the scoring, how ties work, and whether a shut box ends
the game.
_Avoid_: Mode, variant

### People

**Player**:
A colleague on the roster.
_Avoid_: User, member

**Benched**:
A player taken off the picker and the fika rota, whose history stays.
_Avoid_: Deleted, inactive

**Profile**:
One player's page of numbers, badges and games. Your own is also where you set your name, emoji
and song.

**Song**:
A player's chosen music: a YouTube link or an uploaded clip, trimmed to taste. Its **walk-up** is a
few seconds as the player's turn begins; its **anthem** plays when they win.
_Avoid_: Victory song, theme, jingle

**Badge**:
An achievement a player earns from the games they have played.
_Avoid_: Trophy, award

**Fika**:
The weekly duty of buying fika, drawn on Mondays from last week's games. It goes to the player
with the worst average finish among those who have not had a turn yet in the current cycle.

**Team play**:
An event mode for a team day: teams of guests play on their own boards. It is never a Game and
never touches the stats.
